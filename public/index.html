Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: 2026. Plain-language summary, not a substitute for reading it in full.

What we collect

Your username and a hashed (never plain-text) password, plus whatever you enter into the app: workout splits, PRs, bodyweight, your diet profile and generated meal plans, your chosen sport, and any food descriptions you type into the AI Nutritionist.

Third parties

When you log food, your description is matched against a small built-in food database on the server, no external service sees it. If you're using this inside Claude, that request may go to Anthropic instead. Neither is used for advertising, and your data isn't sold or shared with anyone else.

No ads, no tracking

This app doesn't run ads, doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies, and doesn't build a profile of you beyond the app data described above.

Security

Passwords are hashed before storage. Reasonable care is taken to keep your data secure, but this is a small, independently run project, not an enterprise service, and no system can guarantee perfect security.

Your rights

You can permanently delete your account and everything tied to it at any time from within the app (Delete account, in the menu). This removes your login and all your app data straight away, it isn't recoverable afterward.

Children

This app isn't directed at children under 16, and accounts require confirming you're 16 or older. If you believe a child has created an account, contact the site operator to have it removed.

Not professional advice

Workout splits, calorie and macro targets, meal plans, and sport drills are automatically generated, general-purpose suggestions. They are not personalized medical, nutritional, or coaching advice. Talk to a doctor, registered dietitian, or qualified trainer before starting or changing any diet or exercise program, particularly if you have a health condition, injury, or are pregnant.

Use at your own risk

Physical exercise carries an inherent risk of injury. Stop immediately and seek appropriate care if you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort. Calorie and nutrition estimates are approximate and may be inaccurate.

No warranty, limited liability

This service is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the operator of this site is not liable for any injury, loss, or damage arising from its use.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself, you're responsible for activity on your account. Don't use this service for anything unlawful, or to enter someone else's personal data without their consent.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the update.
